# Cards

The 36 Lenormand cards as read in this skill. Each entry: number, English and Chinese name, playing card insert, polarity, core meaning, how the card reads as a person and as an outcome, and a rough timing (used only when the querent explicitly asks "when").

Polarity: **+** positive, **0** neutral (takes its color from neighbors), **−** negative. Heavy cards (Coffin, Scythe, Mountain, Cross, Mice, Snake) carry the most weight when they touch the key position.

The Green Glyphs deck used by the reading table follows the classical numbering and inserts. Its card faces show the English name and number at the foot of each card.

## The 36 cards

**1 Rider / 骑士** · 9♥ · 0
News, a visitor, momentum. Something arrives or starts moving. As a person: a messenger, a young man, someone who comes and goes. As an outcome: the matter starts moving. Timing: days.

**2 Clover / 幸运草** · 6♦ · +
A small piece of luck, an opening that lasts a short time. Lightness after strain. As a person: someone easygoing. As an outcome: a chance worth taking now. Timing: days.

**3 Ship / 船** · 10♠ · 0
Travel, distance, transition, trade, longing for somewhere else. As a person: a traveler, someone abroad. As an outcome: the matter moves on or moves away. Timing: weeks to months; the length of the journey.

**4 House / 房子** · K♥ · +
Home, family, private life, the foundation, security. As a person: a family member, a man of the house. As an outcome: stability, return to the base. Timing: stable; when things settle.

**5 Tree / 树** · 7♥ · 0
Health, vitality, growth over time, roots, ancestry. As a person: someone steady and slow to change. As an outcome: slow growth; check health. Timing: months to years.

**6 Clouds / 云** · K♣ · −
Confusion, uncertainty, missing information, a mood that obscures judgment. The clear side (toward lighter cards) lifts; the dark side (toward heavier cards) thickens. As a person: someone unclear or unreliable. As an outcome: no clear picture yet. Timing: unsettled until the fog lifts.

**7 Snake / 蛇** · Q♣ · −
A winding path, complication, desire, temptation, a rival, a clever or calculating woman. As a person: someone with an agenda. As an outcome: a detour, a complication that needs care. Timing: delays from detours.

**8 Coffin / 棺材** · 9♦ · −
Ending, closure, illness, a low period, something that must be let go. As a person: someone ill or depleted. As an outcome: the matter ends. Timing: when the thing closes on its own.

**9 Bouquet / 花束** · Q♠ · +
A gift, an invitation, appreciation, charm, a pleasant surprise. As a person: someone kind and sociable. As an outcome: a welcome result. Timing: days to a couple of weeks.

**10 Scythe / 镰刀** · J♦ · −
A cut, a sudden decision, an accident, a harvest. Fast and final. As a person: someone decisive or sharp. As an outcome: an abrupt end or a sharp choice. Timing: sudden.

**11 Whip / 鞭子** · J♣ · −
Conflict, argument, repetition, discipline, physical exertion, sport. As a person: someone critical or combative. As an outcome: friction, or a pattern that repeats. Timing: recurring.

**12 Birds / 鸟** · 7♦ · 0
Conversation, calls, gossip, nerves, a pair of people, short trips. As a person: a talkative couple or siblings. As an outcome: talk, or worry. Timing: days to a couple of weeks.

**13 Child / 孩子** · J♠ · +
A beginning, a child, inexperience, something small, a new start. As a person: a child, a young or naive person. As an outcome: a fresh start on a small scale. Timing: a short while; something new.

**14 Fox / 狐狸** · 9♣ · −
Cunning, caution, self-interest, deception, also work and employment. As a person: someone self-serving, or a colleague. As an outcome: check the facts and the people. Timing: hidden; things move under the surface.

**15 Bear / 熊** · 10♣ · 0
Strength, authority, protection, money and reserves, a boss or a mother figure. As a person: a powerful or protective figure. As an outcome: power is on the matter; resources count. Timing: long; power moves slowly.

**16 Stars / 星星** · 6♥ · +
Hope, direction, clarity, a goal coming into view, networks, the online world. As a person: an idealist, a guide. As an outcome: the direction is right. Timing: night; clarity arrives.

**17 Stork / 鹳** · Q♥ · +
Change, relocation, improvement, a new phase, pregnancy in the literal context. As a person: someone in transition. As an outcome: things change for the better. Timing: weeks; a transition window.

**18 Dog / 狗** · 10♥ · +
A friend, loyalty, trust, support. As a person: a loyal friend. As an outcome: help from someone you can trust. Timing: steady.

**19 Tower / 塔** · 6♠ · 0
Institutions, authority, bureaucracy, solitude, boundaries, long life. As a person: an official, someone aloof. As an outcome: the institution decides; distance. Timing: long.

**20 Garden / 花园** · 8♠ · 0
Public life, society, events, gatherings, the audience. As a person: a social person, the public. As an outcome: the matter becomes public or social. Timing: at an event.

**21 Mountain / 山** · 8♣ · −
An obstacle, delay, difficulty, an enemy, isolation. As a person: someone obstructive or distant. As an outcome: blocked for now. Timing: delayed until the block moves.

**22 Paths / 道路** · Q♦ · 0
A choice, a crossroads, options, a decision point. As a person: someone undecided. As an outcome: a decision is needed. Timing: when the choice is made.

**23 Mice / 老鼠** · 7♣ · −
Erosion, loss, worry, theft, stress, slow depletion. As a person: someone who drains. As an outcome: losses; something is eaten away. Timing: gradual.

**24 Heart / 心** · J♥ · +
Love, affection, warmth, generosity, what the heart wants. As a person: someone loving, a romantic interest. As an outcome: love or goodwill is present. Timing: when feelings decide.

**25 Ring / 戒指** · A♣ · 0
Commitment, contract, partnership, a cycle, a promise. As a person: a partner. As an outcome: an agreement binds; the cycle repeats. Timing: recurring, contractual.

**26 Book / 书** · 10♦ · 0
A secret, knowledge, study, the unknown, research. Closed information. As a person: a scholar, someone discreet. As an outcome: not yet known, or must be learned. Timing: unknown.

**27 Letter / 信** · 7♠ · 0
A document, a message, written communication, paperwork. As a person: a writer, a correspondent. As an outcome: it comes in writing. Timing: when the document arrives.

**28 Man / 男人** · A♥ · 0
The male querent, or the main man in the querent's life. Significator for a male querent.

**29 Woman / 女人** · A♠ · 0
The female querent, or the main woman in the querent's life. Significator for a female querent.

**30 Lily / 百合** · K♠ · +
Harmony, peace, maturity, virtue, older people, also sexuality and intimacy. As a person: an older or wise person. As an outcome: calm, settled. Timing: long; later in life.

**31 Sun / 太阳** · A♦ · +
Success, energy, victory, warmth, clarity. The strongest positive card; it lifts its neighbors. As a person: someone confident and warm. As an outcome: success. Timing: soon and definite; daytime.

**32 Moon / 月亮** · 8♥ · +
Recognition, reputation, emotion, intuition, creativity. As a person: a creative or sensitive person. As an outcome: recognition, or feelings decide. Timing: about a month; evening.

**33 Key / 钥匙** · 8♦ · +
A solution, a breakthrough, certainty, importance. Confirms whatever it touches. As a person: someone who opens doors. As an outcome: yes; it happens. Timing: definite.

**34 Fish / 鱼** · K♦ · 0
Money, business, trade, abundance, independence, flow; also drink. As a person: a businessperson, someone independent. As an outcome: resources move. Timing: ongoing.

**35 Anchor / 锚** · 9♠ · 0
Stability, persistence, long-term work, arriving and staying, also being stuck. As a person: someone steady or stubborn. As an outcome: it lasts. Timing: months to years.

**36 Cross / 十字架** · 6♣ · −
Burden, duty, suffering, fate, faith. As a person: someone carrying a weight. As an outcome: a burden that must be carried; it matters deeply. Timing: as long as the burden lasts.

## Combination principles

1. **Order.** In a line, the first card is the subject and the second modifies it. Fox + Heart: deception in affection. Heart + Fox: love that turns false. In a grid, read left to right within a row and top to bottom within a column.
2. **Touching cards modify each other strongly.** Cards two or more positions apart interact only through a technique (diagonal, mirror, knighting, distance).
3. **Two heavy cards side by side compound.** Coffin + Scythe is an abrupt, final ending. Mice + Mountain is depletion that also blocks.
4. **A positive card beside a negative card dilutes both.** Sun + Clouds: clarity breaking through confusion. Clouds + Sun: confusion that is about to clear.
5. **Neutral cards take their color from neighbors.** Ship beside Sun is a good journey; Ship beside Coffin is leaving something behind for good.
6. **Key confirms, Sun strengthens, Clouds blurs, Mountain delays, Mice erodes, Coffin ends.** These six act on whatever they touch.
7. **People cards.** Man, Woman, Child, Dog, Fox, Snake, Bear and Lily can each stand for a person. Decide from context and from the question who each one is, and say so.
8. **Three-card strings.** Read the middle card as the hinge: first card, what it becomes through the middle, what it ends in.
